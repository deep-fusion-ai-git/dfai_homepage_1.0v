Philosophy

Our Perception Philosophy

No autonomous driving technology can be complete with a single sensor alone. Cameras are blocked by harsh weather and backlight, while LiDAR is vulnerable to climate changes.

DeepFusion AI takes highly penetrative 4D imaging radar as the core foundation, fusing it with heterogeneous sensors such as cameras, LiDAR, and X-band radar. By thoroughly analyzing even the minutest reflection data collected by sensors, we complete an all-weather perception engine that never falters under any harsh conditions.

Pillar 01

RAPA-R Deep Learning AI Model

RAPA-R is a core foundation technology developed in-house by DeepFusion AI. It is a peerless model that realized the world's first real-time inference with 360-degree all-round perception and stable object tracking, winning the Best Innovation Award in the AI category at CES 2026 for its technological innovation.

  • Equipped with patented filters for signal attenuation and noise reduction
  • High-speed inference performance of 20+ frames per second
  • 60%+ improved object detection precision compared to legacy low-resolution radars
  • Passed field tests for robustness in harsh weather and dusty conditions
Pillar 02

Perceptive Sensor Fusion

As a Late Fusion technology that integrates heterogeneous sensors such as cameras, LiDAR, and X-band radar, it generates drivable paths by detecting drivable space during driving.

  • Real-time coordinate system alignment between camera pixel data and radar RF data
  • Dynamic weighting network automatically scales down low-confidence sensor data
  • LiDAR point cloud compatible interface provided
Pillar 03

Real-time 3D Mapping

We possess unique SLAM technology that visualizes surrounding environments and obstacles as 3D point cloud maps using only radar measurements without GPS. Through this technology, we generate radar-based HD Maps in real-time, perfectly supporting high-precision location estimation which is the core of indoor/outdoor autonomous valet parking.

  • WebGL-based low-latency rendering framework
  • Visual warning system for obstacle collision risk zones in control rooms
  • 360-degree free-viewport mode supported
Pillar 04

Edge Compute Module

We package and deploy custom low-power edge computing hardware optimized for vehicles and small vessels, enabling standalone onboard inference without high-performance GPU servers.

  • Optimization of GPU/FPGA-based AI models and ultra-low power operation (under 25W)
  • IP67 waterproof and dustproof case packaging complete (conforming to marine & military validation standards)
  • 10Gbps ultra-high speed optical communication sensor packaging and real-time CAN bus communication
Pillar 05

Pre/Post Processing (Denoising, Tracker)

DFAI's highly reliable signal processing and tracking framework secures extremely accurate and seamless real-time obstacle tracking paths through precise raw signal analysis.

  • Raw data preprocessing technology optimized for Radar sensor characteristics (data augmentation)
  • Missing frame synchronization across input sensors and stable estimation for inferred objects
Pillar 06

AI Perception & Auto-Labeling

Based on DFAI's proprietary advanced deep learning model, we precisely perceive even small ground and marine obstacles, and possess an automatic labeling pipeline to efficiently construct large-scale training datasets.

  • Equipped with 3D & 2D high-precision object perception deep learning models based on RAPA-R / RAPA-RC
  • Dataset auto-labeling technology that improves speed by over 90% compared to manual work
  • Possession of large-scale multimodal datasets for real roads (30K) and marine vessel (10K) environments
Pillar 07

Virtual radar & pre training ai model

Through the world's best virtual radar synthesis and training acceleration technologies, we realize perfect accuracy even in extreme physical environments.

  • The world's only 4D imaging radar virtualization technology that secured 600,000+ datasets
  • Two-Way multimodal deep learning models based on RAPA-R and Knowledge Distillation
  • Achieving virtual LiDAR-level (128ch) high-precision perception accuracy through real data fine-tuning
