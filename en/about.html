Our Story

While Others Worry, We Prepare

DeepFusion AI began in fields where the limitations of cameras are most stark, such as in bad weather or dark nights. We focused on radar, which had previously been used as a simple auxiliary sensor.

We independently developed the RAPA-RLC platform, a 4D imaging radar-based perception deep learning algorithm. Currently, we supply all-round perception hardware and software modules spanning defense combat vehicles, maritime USVs, and production autonomous vehicles.

Vision

Global No.1 Radar Perception Solution

We provide high-performance 4D imaging radar deep learning engines leading the autonomous systems and defense unmanned markets. Going beyond harsh weather and the limits of traditional sensors, we present the standard for perfect perception.

Mission

Fused Perception for a Safe and Reliable World

We realize radar-centric sensor fusion going beyond the limits of cameras and LiDAR, maximizing the safety and operational efficiency of next-generation unmanned systems and mobility.
