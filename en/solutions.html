Solution 01

Automotive

Radar-Centric Automotive Perception for Safer Road Environments

DeepFusion AI's Automotive solution fuses diverse sensor data, centering on 4D imaging radar alongside cameras and LiDAR, to reliably perceive objects and spaces around the vehicle. By combining the strengths of each sensor and adjusting the influence of low-confidence data in extreme conditions, it supports continuous object detection and tracking in all weather and lighting environments.

✓ Key Features

3D Object Perception

Accurately perceives various 3D objects including vehicles, pedestrians, and two-wheelers.

Real-Time Trajectory Tracking

Tracks precise target position, velocity, and moving directions in real-time.

Sensor Fusion & Confidence Correction

Fuses radar, camera, and LiDAR, and dynamically adjusts sensor confidence in harsh weather, tunnels, and low light.

Surrounding Environment Analysis

Provides multi-dimensional analysis of drivable spaces and complex road boundaries.

Edge Computing Optimization

Optimized for vehicle onboard edge AI computing units, enabling low-latency integration with ADAS systems.

Solution 02

USV

All-Weather Marine Perception for Unmanned Surface Vehicles

DeepFusion AI's USV perception solution detects and continuously tracks moving objects and marine obstacles around vessels using 4D imaging radar and AI-based signal analysis. By identifying ships, buoys, and floating debris that impact navigation safety, it provides critical perception inputs required for path planning and collision avoidance systems.

✓ Key Features

Marine Small Object Detection

Detects vessels of various sizes and small floating debris to guarantee navigation safety.

Dynamic Target Tracking

Continuously tracks real-time positions, velocities, and directions of moving targets.

Sea Clutter Mitigation

Suppresses sea clutter and water reflections caused by strong waves and rainstorms to maximize reliability.

Multi-Sensor Fusion

Combines radar and camera sensor inputs to achieve comprehensive situational awareness.

System Integration & Model Tuning

Provides interfaces for autonomous navigation systems and optimizes models tailored for maritime climates.

Solution 03

UGV

Robust Unmanned Ground Vehicle Perception in Unstructured Rough Terrains

DeepFusion AI's UGV perception solution recognizes the surrounding situation of the vehicle in various land environments such as unpaved roads, bushes, mud, and slopes through 4D imaging radar-based spatial recognition and object tracking. By delivering detected obstacles and terrain information to the autonomous driving and path planning systems, it supports unmanned ground vehicles to maneuver stably even in complex unstructured environments.

✓ Key Features

Rough Terrain & Unstructured Obstacle Detection

Detects obstacles and drivable paths even in off-road, bush, and muddy environments without lane markings.

Moving Object Position & Status Tracking

Tracks precise target position, velocity, and moving status of dynamic targets.

Slope & Step Recognition

Recognizes slopes, steps, and terrain roughness in real-time to assess vehicle operation limits.

Dust & Low-Light Performance

Maintains clear detection behind bushes, in dense dust/sandstorms, and in complete darkness using 4D radar signal penetration.

3D Space Map & Path Planning Integration

Generates 3D grid spatial maps ahead of the vehicle to feed into autonomous steering and navigation planners.

Solution 04

Waves

Real-Time Surface Analysis for Forward Wave Perception

DeepFusion AI's Waves solution analyzes water surface reflection data collected by radar using AI to detect wave shapes and movements. By providing analyzed wave information to autonomous navigation or pilot assistance systems, it supports safer navigation decisions such as deceleration, route adjustment, and attitude control.

✓ Key Features

Forward Surface & Wave Detection

Identifies water surface state and approaching waves early within 100m in front of the vessel.

Wave Height & Direction Analysis

Analyzes wave height, velocity, and incoming direction in real-time, outputting calculations 10+ times per second.

All-Weather Operations

Guarantees 90%+ measurement accuracy under heavy rain, typhoons, and thick night fog.

Risk Assessment Data Provision

Provides critical danger indexes regarding exceptional waves to trigger auto-steering or speed reductions.

Control System Integration

Fully integrates with autonomous marine computers and vessel motion stabilizers.

Solution 05

AMR

Autonomous Mobile Robot Perception & Localization for Industrial Sites

DeepFusion AI's AMR solution recognizes dynamic objects and fixed structures around robots in indoor and outdoor industrial environments through 4D imaging radar-based object perception and localization technology. By integrating perception results with the robot's mapping, localization, path planning, and collision prevention systems, it supports autonomous mobile robots to operate stably in complex industrial environments.

✓ Key Features

Worker, Forklift & Equipment Detection

Detects moving operators, forklifts, and equipment around warehouses and assembly lines without blind spots.

Dynamic Object Path Tracking

Traces moving obstacles and projects their trajectory 360° to preemptively prevent collisions.

Radar-Based SLAM

Maintains precise localization using radar signal traits even where lidar or optical cameras fail.

Real-Time Mapping & Updates

Dynamically constructs and updates 3D maps in warehouses where cargo layouts shift constantly.

Harsh Environment Mitigation

Bypasses challenges from dense smoke, dust, and lighting fluctuations to feed data directly to path planning and safety logic.
